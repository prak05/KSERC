KSERC AI Truing-Up Tool
An AI-Driven Innovation for Regulatory Excellence
🎯 What We Built
An intelligent system that automates Kerala's electricity tariff-setting process (truing-up). It combines AI-powered document analysis with KSERC's regulatory framework to process complex financial petitions in 2-4 weeks instead of 6-12 months.
⏳ The Challenge We Solve
Manual & Slow
Truing-up took 6-12 months. Officers manually reviewed 500+ page petitions.
Complex Rules
KSERC regulations (12+ rules, multiple exceptions) applied inconsistently.
Data Entry Errors
Thousands of cost items manually copied from PDFs. High error risk.
Lack of Transparency
Limited audit trail. Decisions not fully documented or explainable.
🔄 How It Works (3 Steps)
1. Extract: Upload PDF → AI automatically extracts cost heads, revenue, and financials
2. Analyze: AI applies all KSERC regulations & flags deviations with reasoning
3. Review & Report: Officers review, override if needed, then generate formal orders
✨ Key Features
  • Automated PDF Extraction – No manual data entry
  • Smart Prudence Checks – All regulations applied instantly
  • Human Override – Officers maintain full control
  • Auto Report Generation – Formal KSERC orders (PDF/DOCX)
  • Full Audit Trail – Complete transparency & accountability
  • Secure Storage – Supabase + encryption, local-first option
Impact: 6-12 month process → 2-4 weeks | 100% regulatory compliance | Full transparency for citizens & courts

❓ Common Questions
Can the AI make mistakes?
Yes. That's why it's "AI-assisted, human-verified." Every AI decision is flagged for officer review. Officers can override with documented reasoning. Full audit trail maintained.
Is our data safe?
All data encrypted in Supabase. PDFs not permanently stored—only extracted data retained. Full audit logs. Option for on-premise deployment with zero external dependencies.
How are KSERC regulations applied?
Hardcoded rules: Electricity duty = ₹0, CSR/RPO = ₹0, O&M capped per Reg 12(3), 2/3 revenue efficiency share, AT&C loss benchmarks. When regulations change, we update the rules.
What if PDF extraction fails?
Fallback chain: Claude → Gemini → Groq → GitHub Models → OpenRouter. If all fail, system alerts officer. Manual data entry option available with "manually verified" marking.
Can officers override AI decisions?
Yes. Simple "Modify Value" click. New amount with reasoning required. Timestamped, attributed to officer, reversible, full audit trail preserved.
How different from spreadsheets?
Spreadsheets need manual copy-paste. Our tool auto-reads PDFs, understands context, provides reasoning, maintains audit trail, generates documents instantly, and scales to all licensees simultaneously.
